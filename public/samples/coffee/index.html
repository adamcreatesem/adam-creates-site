The ritual

More Than a Drink

Washed beans, roasted over the coals, ground by hand — then poured in three rounds: abol, tona, baraka.

  1. Wash the beans in the open flame's light
  2. Roast until they sing
  3. Grind with the stone
  4. Brew in the jebena, pour three rounds
