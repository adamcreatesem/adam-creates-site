Our Story

Food With Time In It

Mesob Nights was built for the way people actually eat in Addis -- gathered around one big plate, sharing, talking, staying long enough for the coffee to come out. Every dish on the menu is made from scratch, including the injera, which is baked fresh each day in the back.

Traditional menu. Doro wat, kitfo, and a fasting platter that covers vegetarian and vegan needs. No fusion, no shortcuts -- the flavors are the ones people expect from a real Ethiopian table.

"We sat for three hours and nobody rushed us. Coffee came at the end, the way it should."

The Ceremony

Coffee, the Way It Ends Every Table

Coffee ceremony. The visit ends with a full jebena ceremony -- the beans are roasted at your table, the coffee is poured from the clay pot, and popcorn comes with it. That is the part people remember.

Three rounds, the way it is done at home: abol, tona and baraka. Take them slow. Nobody here is in a hurry, and that is the point.
