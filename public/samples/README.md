# Concept Samples: fictional businesses

These are **fictional sample businesses** we built to show what we can make
per business type. They are NOT real businesses: names, logos, addresses,
and contact info are invented, and photos are generic stock (Unsplash, free
license). No consent needed; safe to show publicly.

Real, pitch-ready builds (Tikka Hub, Al Baraka, etc.) live in
`freelance/clients/client-websites/` and stay private until each business
says yes.

## Strategy (per Adam, Aug 16)
- **Sample sites are the easy, scalable part:** build many types fast.
- **Systems are the deep work:** management software (school/student mgmt,
  employee mgmt, hospital, orgs, small/large teams) gets our time.
- The Adam Creates website shows these fictional samples as concept cards,
  each openable in a mini-window preview.

## Systems
- **Clinic Management System** (`clinic-system/`): interactive demo:
  booking, patient records, reminders, stock/expiry alerts. Data resets on
  reload. This is the template for the "deep work": a real deployment gets
  a backend (e.g., Supabase/Appwrite) + WhatsApp notifications.

## Design principle
Every sample has its own visual identity, no shared templates:
- Restaurant: Bodoni Moda serif, mesob warmth
- Travel: parchment + stamps + dashed routes (DM Serif + Space Mono)
- Gym: brutalist black + volt green (Archivo Black)
- Pharmacy: clinical white + green (Work Sans)
- Coffee: ceremony browns + jebena (Fraunces)
- Wedding: ivory + gold, script (Cormorant + Great Vibes)
- Auto: steel + safety orange, hazard stripes (Oswald + Space Mono)
- Creator: dark dashboard, neon stats (Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono)
- Retail: fashion editorial, Italiana serif
