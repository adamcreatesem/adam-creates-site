# Sample Imagery Credits

Photo credits for the **concept sample** pages under `public/samples/` (not the Adam Creates site itself).

Real photographs are sourced from **Wikimedia Commons**; each row links to the exact source page, where the author and full licence terms are recorded. Local filenames are listed so each file matches one-to-one.

The rest of the imagery is **concept art — either AI-generated via our internal Gemini pipeline or carried over from an earlier design pass** — used for fictional businesses where no fitting free photo exists. None of it is linked to an external source, and none depicts a real person, place or establishment.

> Note: these samples are demo pages shown to potential clients. If a photo ships on a public or client site, keep this list reachable (footer line or an `/credits` page) so the free licences are honoured.

### How this list was verified

Every Commons row above was confirmed by **perceptual-hash matching** (the on-disk file compared against current Commons candidates of the same search terms, distance 0–2 of 64 bits) and cross-checked by **vision model descriptions** (Gemini via Composio) against what the sample page claims the photo shows. Files that could not be matched to a live Commons source are listed as concept imagery rather than risk a wrong attribution.
