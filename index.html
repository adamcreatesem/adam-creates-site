FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to the things people usually ask before we start working together.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the scope. A website takes 1 to 3 weeks, branding 1 to 2 weeks, and other projects vary. I'll give you a clear timeline during our first chat.

How does pricing work?

Fixed pricing for defined projects, or hourly for ongoing work. Payment is split in two, 50% to start and 50% on completion. Everything is agreed upfront, no surprises.

Can we work together remotely?

Absolutely. I work with clients worldwide. We'll communicate via email or video calls, whatever works best for you.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes, every project includes revision rounds. I work closely with you throughout so the final result is exactly what you envisioned.

What do you need from me to get started?

For websites: your goals, brand assets, and content. For branding: your vision and target audience. I'll guide you through everything.
